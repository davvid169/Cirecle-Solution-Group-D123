About us

Our history

From a small software company in Amersfoort to an international organisation.

2005

Circle Solutions was founded in Amersfoort by software engineer Raymond Jacobs and project manager Elleke Smit. The company began by developing custom software for local logistics companies.

2010s

Circle Solutions expanded beyond logistics into healthcare and education, while continuing to focus on practical, reliable, and user-friendly software.

2018

The company launched Circle D.O.T.S. (Digital Office Tools & Solutions), its own modular platform designed to help organisations streamline their internal processes.

Today

Circle Solutions now has more than 40 employees and serves clients in the Netherlands, Belgium, and Germany.
