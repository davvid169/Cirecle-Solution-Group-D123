Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions about working with Circle Solutions.

What types of software do you build?

We build custom software for companies in logistics, healthcare, and education. We also offer our own platform, Circle D.O.T.S., for internal process management.

How does a typical project start?

Every project starts with a discovery session where we get to understand your team, your processes, and your goals. From there we propose an approach and timeline.

Do you work with companies outside the Netherlands?

Yes. We work with clients in the Netherlands, Belgium, and Germany, and are open to international projects.

What happens after the software is delivered?

We offer ongoing support and maintenance contracts. We believe good software is a long-term relationship, not a one-off delivery.

Can you integrate with our existing systems?

In most cases, yes. Integration with existing tools and databases is a standard part of how we work.

How long does a project typically take?

This depends on the scope. Smaller projects may take 2–3 months; larger ones 6–12 months. We always give you a clear timeline after the discovery phase.

Do you have any other questions?

Choose the channel that works best for you and contact us. We'll get back to you within 24 hours at the latest.
