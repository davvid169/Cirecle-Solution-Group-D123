Privacy policy

We believe privacy should be simple and understandable. This policy explains what this website stores and how you can contact us about your data.

Who we are

Circle Solutions B.V. is a custom software company based in Amersfoort, the Netherlands. For privacy questions, contact us at hi@circlesolutions.com.

Information on this website

This website does not use advertising cookies or analytics trackers. Your language preference and any guestbook entry you submit are stored locally in your browser. The contact form shown on this website does not currently transmit or store its contents.

Local storage and guestbook

Language preferences and guestbook entries remain on your device through browser localStorage. You can remove them at any time by clearing this site's browser data. Do not include confidential or sensitive personal information in a guestbook entry.

Contact and your rights

If you contact us directly by email or phone, we use the information you provide only to respond to your enquiry and manage our relationship with you. You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing hi@circlesolutions.com.
