Privacy policy

We believe privacy should be simple and understandable. This policy explains which data this website collects, why we need it and what your rights are.

Who we are

Circle Solutions B.V. is a custom software company based in Amersfoort, the Netherlands. We are responsible for the personal data collected through this website. For privacy questions, contact us at info@circlesolutions.com. hi@circlesolutions.com.

What data we collect

We only collect the data you give us yourself. Through the contact form we receive your name, email address and message, which we use to answer your question. Through the guestbook we receive your name, company name and message, which are shown on the guestbook page.

Cookies and storage

This website does not use advertising cookies or analytics trackers. A session cookie keeps the guestbook working during your visit, and your language choice (EN or NL) is saved in your browser. You can remove both at any time by clearing your browser data.

Your rights

We never sell your data and only use it for the reason you gave it to us. Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data by emailing info@circlesolutions.com. You can also file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). hi@circlesolutions.com.
